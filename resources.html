Data

  • Level 2 order book data trials from WRDS. WRDS is inviting faculty to trial the NYSE and ARCA level 2 order book data (TAQ Integrated Feed) and collecting interest in subscribing. To join the subscription wish list or seek additional information, contact WRDS. See also the data manual, Battalio et al. (2021), and Holden et al. (2023).
  • Quote-to-trade ratios from Quoting activity and the cost of capital by Ioanid Rosu, Elvira Sojli, and Wing Wah Tham. WRDS data was used in preparing this output.

WRDS-TME Prize

Recognizing strong work presented at TME.

The WRDS-TME Best Paper Award recognizes papers presented at TME that make a particularly strong contribution to market microstructure research.

Generously sponsored by WRDS

The WRDS-TME Best Paper Award

Every academic year, all papers presented at TME are candidates for the award. A selected group of reviewers votes on the best paper of the year. The winning paper authors receive a monetary reward of $3,000 and an award trophy.

Past winners

  • 2025-26, Is 24/7 Trading Better?, by Patrick Blonien and Alexander Ober (recording, paper)
  • 2024-25, Optimal Exchange Design, by Daniel Chen (recording, paper)
  • 2023-24, Measuring Public and Private Information using Quote and Trade Races, by James Brugler and Terry Hendershott (paper)
  • 2022-23, Would Order-By-Order Auctions Be Competitive?, by Thomas Ernst, Chester S. Spatt, and Jian Sun

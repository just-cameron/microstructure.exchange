Farshid Abdi
Overnight Corporate Bond Trading
with Marco Rossi and Botao Wu
September 15, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. ET.

paper Zoom
Abstract

Corporate bond trades executed after 6:30 p.m. and before 8:00 a.m. are not publicly disseminated through TRACE until the following morning, creating a recurring interval of delayed post-trade transparency. Using enhanced TRACE data, the paper studies how this temporary opacity affects trading and price discovery in the corporate bond market.
